Our Story

What is Desi Drip?

Desi Drip is a contemporary artisanal cafe celebrating the rich, vibrant heritage of South Asian coffee and tea traditions fused with modern specialty brewing.

From our signature slow-drip single-origin coffees and aromatic spiced chai infusions to handcrafted plant-based pastries and gourmet fusion desserts, every creation is brewed with soul and served with warm hospitality. Whether you're grabbing your morning wake-up drip or catching up with friends over decadent treats, make sure you don't miss us!
